    How to start

    1. Open your ELN Entry

    Click to open the ELN tab. Click and customize a reaction code to create a new entry. Beware that once entry is created it cannot be deleted and the reaction code cannot be modified . To hide the unwanted entries on the overview of ELN simply click in `Hide column on the right.

    2. Customize name of entry in 'Title'

    On the page of the ELN entry enter a title of your choice for the reaction and click . From now on your entry would be automatically saved regularly. But it is still strongly recommended to save regularly after each ammendment!

    3. Add your reaction and reagents

    Method 1 (recommended): First data entry then chemical equation

    It saves your time from drawing chemical structures.

    1. Look up the CAS number either on GESTIS (in German) or on CAS Sci Finder (in English, login with name@uni-jena.de required)
    2. Paste the CAS-Number into a cell in the column code and hit Enter on keyboard.
    3. In the pop-up window click the chemical of choice once to select. Usually the fields in Name, mf (molecular formula), mw (molecular weight) and density are automatically filled up. Nevertheless for some molecules, of which relatively little literature are currently available, manual input might be required.
    4. Click the to add the molecules into your chemical editor. Use the Select Tool in the toolbar (free selection as shown in video or double click a bond or an atom of the molecule) to rearrange the components in the chemical equation. Products stand on the right of the reaction arrow with a e.g. 'P1' watermark.
    5. Use the Clean Up Tool to clean up the equation.
